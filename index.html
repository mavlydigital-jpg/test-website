The real problem

You don't need more leads. You need to stop losing them.

Most waterproofing contractors pour money into ads while half their phone calls go straight to voicemail. Then they wonder why nothing's working. Here's what's actually happening.

01

62% of your calls die

The average home services contractor misses more than half their inbound calls. Those homeowners don't try you again. They call whoever picks up first, and the next contractor in the search results wins.

02

You take 6 hours to call back

By then they've had two other contractors quote them. First responder wins about 60% of the time. Sixth responder wins basically nothing. That stat is brutal but it's true.

03

Old leads sit in your phone

Every quote you gave 60 days ago that "didn't decide yet"? About 1 in 10 would book this week if someone followed up. Almost nobody follows up. That's $20K to $40K of dead jobs sitting in your CRM right now.

04

You can't see what's working

You're spending $1K to $3K a month on ads and you can't tell which keywords actually brought you closed jobs. So you keep paying for the broken half and don't know which half it is.

05

Reviews die unasked

Happy customers leave reviews when you ask them on day 1, not day 30. Most contractors never ask. That's why your competitor with 200 reviews keeps showing up above you in local search.

06

You hate season swings

Crews busy in May, sitting idle in November. Cash flow looks like a heart attack on a chart. Most agencies make this worse by only running ads when seasonal demand is already high.

More ads on top of a broken system just bleed money faster.

How it works

Four phases. In this order.

We diagnose first. Then we build. Then we launch. Then we grow. Most agencies skip the first two phases because they don't get paid for them. That's why most agencies lose their clients in three months.

Phase 1
Week 1

Discover

We pull every number from your last 90 days. Call logs, ad spend, GHL pipeline, GBP analytics, missed calls. One report. Real numbers. No PowerPoint slides.

  • Listen to 20-30 of your recent inbound calls

Phase 2

Build

We install the system that stops calls from dying before a single new dollar goes to Google. This pays for the next 60 days of work by itself.

Hard questions

The honest answers to what you're actually thinking.

I've been burned by agencies before. Why are you different?

Fair question. Most agencies sell you Facebook ads, take a retainer, and disappear after the first invoice. Mavly doesn't take a retainer. We charge per qualified call. If your phone isn't ringing, we're not making money. That alignment fixes about 80% of the agency horror stories.

What's a "qualified call" exactly?

Any inbound phone call from a homeowner that lasts 2 minutes or more. The 2-minute rule means we only bill when it's a real conversation, not a butt-dial or a robocall. If a call ends in 30 seconds, you don't pay. If a wrong number gets through, you don't pay. We can dispute borderline cases together.

What does "Ignition" actually cover?

It's the one-time fee that pays for us to install the entire system: Google Ads account setup, campaign build, automation workflows in GHL, dashboard configuration, GBP optimization, tracking integration. About 30-40 hours of build work compressed into your first week. After Ignition, you only ever pay per qualified call.

Does this actually work for waterproofing? It's so seasonal.

Yes. Spring and storm season is where the heavy money is and we push hard there. Fall and winter we shift to crawl space encapsulation, foundation crack repair before freeze, and reactivating your old leads. Most contractors do $80K in May and $12K in November. We help smooth that to something closer to $40K every month.

What if I'm a small shop just getting started?

If you're under $10K/month, we'll probably tell you to come back in 6 months. The Google Ads spend alone needs $1,500/month to work, and it's hard to justify that if you're not already covering rent and trucks. We'd rather lose your sale than sign you up and watch you struggle.

I get all my work from referrals. Do I need this?

Referrals are the best leads any contractor gets. But they're unpredictable and you can't control the timing. Mavly fills the gaps between referrals so your crews aren't sitting idle 3 days a week. The review and reactivation system also feeds more referrals back to you over time. Nobody's saying replace what works. We add what's missing.

What's the catch with the $497 founding price?

No catch. The first 3 contractors who sign get the lower Ignition fee in exchange for case study rights, meaning we can use your real numbers in our marketing once you start seeing results. After the first 3 spots fill, Ignition goes to $1,497. That's it. No bait and switch.

Do you guarantee a number of jobs or calls?

No. The honest answer is no agency doing this work seriously guarantees a number. What we guarantee is the system: the 30-second text back, the 5-touch follow-up, the dashboard showing every call, and 90 days then cancel anytime by text. If it stops working, you leave. That's a stronger commitment than any "ROI guarantee" you'll see elsewhere.

Who pays for the Google ads?

You do, directly to Google with your own card. We never touch your ad budget. You see every dollar in your own Google Ads account, which is in your name. We just run the campaign for you. That's the only way you know we're not marking up your ad spend.

What happens if I cancel?

After the 90-day commitment, you can cancel any time by text. Within 24 hours we pause the ads, remove our access to your Google Ads account, and stop billing. You keep your Google Ads account, your GBP, your phone numbers, your reviews, your CRM. Nothing gets locked or held hostage.
