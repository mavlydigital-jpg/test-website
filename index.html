Straight answers

Questions you are right to ask

  • Do you generate leads?

    Yes, but not where we start. First we make sure your business captures the opportunities it already gets. Then we help create more qualified demand.

  • Why not just run Google or Facebook ads?

    You can. But if the leads you already get aren't handled properly, more leads just make the leak bigger. We fix the process first, then scale acquisition.

  • Does this replace my employees?

    No. Your team still handles sales, appointments and jobs. The system just makes sure fewer opportunities disappear because someone was busy or forgot to follow up.

  • What if our follow-up is already good?

    Good. We'll look for other leaks. If there isn't a meaningful problem, you may not need us.

  • Is this an AI system?

    Technology is part of the mechanism, but that's not the product. The product is more opportunities turning into real conversations, appointments and jobs.

  • What if I need more leads?

    That's the second layer. Once your process works, we build and manage acquisition to bring more qualified homeowners into it.
