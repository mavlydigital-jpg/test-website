  • New inquiries

Frequently asked questions

Questions you are right to ask

  • Do you generate leads?

    Yes, but that's not where we start. We start by making sure your business can properly capture the opportunities it's already getting. Once that's working, we can help generate more qualified demand.

    You can. But if the leads you're already getting aren't being handled properly, adding more leads can simply make the leak bigger. We fix the process first. Then we scale acquisition.
